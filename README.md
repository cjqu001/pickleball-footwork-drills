# Pickleball Footwork Drills

This repository holds short, practical drills for moving better during real pickleball points. It focuses on balance, timing, and recovery, not general fitness.

## Start here

- [10-minute solo plan](plans/10-minute-solo.md) - rehearse movement patterns alone.
- [20-minute partner plan](plans/20-minute-partner.md) - add contact timing, controlled shots, and random feeds.
- [Footwork basics](docs/footwork-basics.md) - the terms used in every drill.
- [Drill index](drills/README.md) - all drill cards in one list.
- [Resources](docs/resources.md) - the source register and optional further reading.

Short on time? Open the solo plan and follow the blocks in order.

## How to use these drills

- Movement quality comes first. Balanced contact and timely recovery come before speed.
- Add speed only when you can hold good balance on most reps.
- Solo work rehearses a pattern. It does not test how fast you react to a real opponent.
- Partner work with random feeds is the closest match to real points.
- Record a few numbers after each session with the [practice log](templates/practice-log.md).

## What this repository is

- The drill cards and plans are original instructional summaries and original practice suggestions.
- Each drill card states its sources and whether it is an original drill or an original adaptation.
- External resources are linked, not copied. Paid books are optional further reading. You do not need to buy anything to use these drills.
- The practice doses are suggestions for practice. They are not proven by research.
- These drills are not scientifically validated, and they do not promise a rating improvement.

## Safety

Practice on a clear, dry court. Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.
