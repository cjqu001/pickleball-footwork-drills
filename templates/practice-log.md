## What each column means

- Date - the day you practiced.
- Drill - the drill ID and version, such as `FW-05 easier`.
- Dose - what you actually did, such as sets and reps.
- Success count - the number from the drill's success check, such as 8/10.
- One observed mistake - the single most common mistake you noticed.
- Next-session focus - one thing to change next time.
