# 10-Minute Solo Plan

Use this plan to rehearse movement patterns on your own. Solo work builds the pattern. It does not test how fast you react to a real opponent. For that, use the [20-minute partner plan](20-minute-partner.md).

Equipment: Paddle, a few balls, and up to 4 optional markers.

This plan is an original practice suggestion. The times and doses are not proven by research.

## Before you start

- Practice on a clear, dry court.
- Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.
- Movement quality comes first. Stay balanced before you try to go faster.

## Session blocks

| Time | Minutes | Block | Drill and version | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-1:00 | 1 | Setup and easy movement | None | Place markers. Walk, then jog lightly along the kitchen line and back. |
| 1:00-3:00 | 2 | Split step | [FW-01](../drills/01-split-step-timing.md), solo rehearsal (step 1) | 2 sets of 10 split steps. 30 seconds rest between sets. |
| 3:00-4:30 | 1.5 | Kitchen shuffle | [FW-02](../drills/02-kitchen-lateral-shuffle.md), solo rehearsal | 1 set of 10 shuffles to the markers and back. Rest for the remaining time. |
| 4:30-6:00 | 1.5 | Shuffle or crossover | [FW-05](../drills/05-crossover-and-recovery.md), solo rehearsal | 1 set of 8 moves. Shuffle to near markers. Crossover to far markers. Recover each time. |
| 6:00-7:30 | 1.5 | Forward and stop | [FW-04](../drills/04-transition-stop-and-reset.md), solo rehearsal | 3 walking or jogging runs from baseline to kitchen line. Split step at each stop. |
| 7:30-9:00 | 1.5 | Lob turn | [FW-06](../drills/06-turn-and-retrieve-lobs.md), no-ball rehearsal (step 1) | 6 turns, alternating sides. Walk back between reps. |
| 9:00-10:00 | 1 | Cool-down and log | [Practice log](../templates/practice-log.md) | Walk slowly. Fill in one log row per drill. |

Total: 1 + 2 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1 = 10 minutes.

The doses here are shorter than the full doses on the drill cards so they fit in 10 minutes.

## What to record

Write one row per drill in the [practice log](../templates/practice-log.md):

- Date
- Drill and version
- Dose you actually did
- Success count, if the drill has one you can judge alone
- One observed mistake
- Next-session focus

Solo success counts are self-judged. Count balanced landings or balanced stops out of 10.
