# 20-Minute Partner Plan

Use this plan with one partner who feeds balls. It covers contact timing, movement, controlled shots, and random feeds. One player trains and one player feeds. Swap roles in another session, or repeat the plan with roles swapped if you have 40 minutes.

Equipment: Two paddles, a bucket or bag of balls, and up to 3 optional markers.

This plan is an original practice suggestion. The times and doses are not proven by research.

## Before you start

- Practice on a clear, dry court. Clear loose balls between blocks.
- Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.
- Movement quality comes first. The feeder waits until the player is balanced before each feed. Speed up only when movement stays clean.

## Session blocks

| Time | Minutes | Block | Drill and version | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-2:00 | 2 | Setup and easy movement | None | Place markers. Both players walk, then jog lightly, and trade a few easy dinks. |
| 2:00-5:00 | 3 | Contact timing | [FW-01](../drills/01-split-step-timing.md), partner cue (step 2) then read the paddle (step 3) | 1 set of 10 feeds with the voice cue. 1 set of 10 feeds without it. 30 seconds rest between. |
| 5:00-8:00 | 3 | Kitchen shuffle | [FW-02](../drills/02-kitchen-lateral-shuffle.md), standard paddle stage | 2 sets of 10 feeds, alternating sides. 30 seconds rest between. |
| 8:00-10:00 | 2 | Spacing | [FW-03](../drills/03-adjustment-steps-and-spacing.md), standard | 1 set of 10 mixed feeds. Rest for the remaining time. |
| 10:00-13:00 | 3 | Transition | [FW-04](../drills/04-transition-stop-and-reset.md), standard (or easier if stops are late) | 3 runs from baseline to kitchen line, 3 feeds per run. 30 seconds rest between runs. |
| 13:00-14:00 | 1 | Rest | None | Water. Collect balls. |
| 14:00-18:00 | 4 | Random feeds | [FW-07](../drills/07-random-feed-and-recovery.md), standard | 2 sets of 10 random feeds. 45 seconds rest between. |
| 18:00-20:00 | 2 | Log | [Practice log](../templates/practice-log.md) | Both players agree on the success counts. Fill in one row per drill. |

Total: 2 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 1 + 4 + 2 = 20 minutes.

The doses here are shorter than the full doses on the drill cards so they fit in 20 minutes.

## Swapping drills

- If wide balls are a problem, replace the FW-03 block with [FW-05](../drills/05-crossover-and-recovery.md), standard, for the same 2 minutes.
- If lobs are a problem, replace the FW-04 block with [FW-06](../drills/06-turn-and-retrieve-lobs.md), predictable feeds (step 2), for the same 3 minutes.
- Keep the block times the same so the plan still totals 20 minutes.

## What to record

Write one row per drill in the [practice log](../templates/practice-log.md):

- Date
- Drill and version
- Dose you actually did
- Success count from the drill card, such as balanced contacts out of 10
- One observed mistake
- Next-session focus

The feeder judges the success counts. Keep judgments simple and consistent.
