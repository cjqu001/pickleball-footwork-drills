# FW-02: Kitchen Lateral Shuffle

## Overview

- ID: FW-02
- Movement skill: Lateral shuffle along the kitchen line.
- Purpose: Reach short balls to either side while staying balanced and facing the net, then return to a useful position for the next dink.
- Experience level: Players who can make a soft dink most of the time. New players can use the catch stage.
- Format: Partner. Solo rehearsal is possible.
- Equipment: Paddle, balls, and 2 optional markers.
- Court area: Kitchen line on both sides of the net.

## Setup

- The player stands in ready position just behind their kitchen line, near the middle of one half of the court.
- Optional: Place a marker about 2 shuffle steps to each side of the player's start spot on the kitchen line.
- The feeder stands at their own kitchen line, directly across the net.
- Feeds are soft underhand tosses or dinks that land in the player's kitchen, 1 to 2 shuffle steps to the left or right.
- The feeder and player are about 14 to 15 feet apart. Do not feed dinks from the baseline.

## Steps

1. Start in ready position behind the kitchen line.
2. The feeder sends a soft ball to one side.
3. Split step as the feeder contacts or releases the ball.
4. Shuffle 1 or 2 steps toward the ball. Keep the chest facing the net and feet from crossing.
5. Catch the ball in front of the body with the non-paddle hand (catch stage), or dink it back to the feeder (paddle stage).
6. Shuffle back toward the start spot and stop balanced before the next feed.
7. The rep ends when the player is set in ready position.

## Suggested practice dose

- 3 sets of 10 feeds, alternating sides.
- 30 to 45 seconds rest between sets.
- About 5 minutes total.
- Solo rehearsal: 2 sets of 10 shuffles without a ball, touching each marker with the paddle.
- This is a suggested practice dose. It is not proven by research.

## Coaching cues

- "Step, slide, stay low."
- "Chest to the net."
- "Stop before they hit again."

## Common mistakes

- Feet click together or cross - keep a gap between the feet the whole time.
- Standing up tall while moving - keep knees bent. Your head should stay at about the same height.
- Reaching with the arm instead of moving the feet - take the extra shuffle step so contact is in front.
- Drifting forward into the kitchen - stop behind the line. Use the line as a reference.
- Not recovering before the next feed - the feeder waits until the player is set. Speed up only when recovery is clean.

## Versions

- Easier: Catch only. The feeder calls the side before each feed.
- Harder: The feeder sends random sides and sometimes a wider ball that needs 3 steps. The player chooses shuffle or a short crossover based on distance (see [FW-05](05-crossover-and-recovery.md)). The feeder also shifts left or right along their kitchen line, and the player recovers to a spot opposite the feeder's new position rather than the original start spot.

## Success check

- Count balanced returns out of 10. A balanced return means the dink or catch was made with feet set, then the player stopped balanced before the next feed.
- Practice goal: 7 out of 10 in the paddle stage.
