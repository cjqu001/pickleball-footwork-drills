# FW-05: Crossover and Recovery

## Overview

- ID: FW-05
- Movement skill: Choosing a shuffle or a crossover based on distance, then recovering under control.
- Purpose: Cover a wide ball efficiently, then get back to a useful position before the next ball. Learn when a shuffle is enough and when it is too slow.
- Experience level: Players who are comfortable with [FW-02](02-kitchen-lateral-shuffle.md).
- Format: Partner. Solo rehearsal is possible.
- Equipment: Paddle, balls, and 4 optional markers.
- Court area: Mid-court or a few steps behind the kitchen line on one half of the court.

## Setup

- The player starts in ready position near the middle of one half of the court, a few steps behind the kitchen line.
- Optional: Place two "near" markers about 1 to 2 steps to each side, and two "far" markers about 4 to 5 steps to each side.
- The feeder stands across the net at their kitchen line.
- Feeds are soft, high-bouncing balls. Near feeds land close to a near marker. Far feeds land near a far marker, still inside the court.

## Steps

1. Start in ready position.
2. Split step as the feeder contacts the ball.
3. Near ball: shuffle 1 or 2 steps, set feet, and hit a soft shot back.
4. Far ball: turn the hips toward the ball, cross the far leg over, and take running steps if needed. Slow down and set the feet before contact. Hit a soft shot back.
5. Recover toward the middle of the area you are covering. Use a crossover first if you are far away, then shuffle as you get close.
6. Stop balanced in ready position before the feeder sends the next ball.
7. The rep ends when you are set.

## Suggested practice dose

- 3 sets of 8 feeds. Mix near and far feeds.
- 45 seconds rest between sets.
- About 5 minutes total.
- Solo rehearsal: Move to each marker without a ball. Shuffle to near markers. Crossover to far markers. Recover each time. 2 sets of 8.
- This is a suggested practice dose. It is not proven by research.

## Coaching cues

- "Near, shuffle. Far, cross."
- "Turn the hips, then go."
- "Cross out, shuffle in."

## Common mistakes

- Shuffling to a far ball and arriving late - turn the hips and use a crossover when the ball is more than about 2 steps away.
- Crossing over for a near ball and overrunning it - use a shuffle when 1 or 2 steps is enough.
- Hitting while still running - take a last small step to slow down before contact.
- Recovering with a slow shuffle from far away - use a crossover first, then switch to a shuffle near the end.
- Recovering all the way to one fixed spot even when the next ball will come from elsewhere - in the harder version, recover based on where the feeder is.

## Versions

- Easier: The feeder calls "near" or "far" and the side before each feed. The player catches instead of hitting.
- Harder: Random near and far feeds with no call. The feeder moves along their kitchen line between feeds, and the player recovers to a spot that covers the feeder's new position.

## Success check

- Count correct movement choices out of 10. Correct means shuffle on near balls, crossover or run on far balls, and a balanced stop before the next feed.
- Practice goal: 8 out of 10.
