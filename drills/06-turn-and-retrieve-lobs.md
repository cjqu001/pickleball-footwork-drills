# FW-06: Turn and Retrieve Lobs

## Overview

- ID: FW-06
- Movement skill: Turn-and-run movement for a lob.
- Purpose: Get back to a lob safely and quickly by turning and running, not backpedaling. Keep track of your partner while you do it.
- Experience level: All players for the no-ball stage. Add feeds once the turn is smooth and controlled.
- Format: Solo rehearsal, then partner feeds.
- Equipment: Paddle, balls, and 1 optional marker.
- Court area: Kitchen line to behind the baseline on one side. Check that the area behind the baseline is clear.

## Setup

- The player starts in ready position just behind their kitchen line.
- Optional: Place a marker 3 to 4 steps inside the baseline, a little to one side. This is the target area to run toward.
- Check the space behind the court. Remove loose balls and bags. Make sure there is a safe run-off.
- For feeds, the feeder stands across the net at mid-court. Feeds are soft, moderate lobs over the player's paddle-side or non-paddle-side shoulder. They should land well inside the baseline.
- If a doubles partner joins, they start at the kitchen line beside the player.

## Steps

1. No-ball rehearsal. From ready position, turn the hips and shoulders to one side. Run forward along a path beside where the ball would land. Slow down near the marker, turn to face the net, and stop balanced. Walk back to start.
2. Predictable feeds. The feeder calls the side, then lobs. The player turns, runs beside the ball's path, and tracks it over the shoulder. Let it bounce. Hit a high, soft ball back deep to the feeder's side.
3. Random feeds. The feeder lobs to either side without calling it. The player reads the lob, turns the right way, and runs.
4. Partner awareness. With a doubles partner, the player whose side the lob crosses calls "mine" or "switch." On "switch," the partner moves across to cover the open side.
5. The rep ends when the player has hit the ball back and moved forward to a balanced ready position, or has reset in the backcourt if there is not time to move forward.

## Suggested practice dose

- No-ball: 2 sets of 6 turns, alternating sides. Walk back between reps. About 2 minutes.
- Feeds: 2 sets of 6 lobs per stage. 45 to 60 seconds rest between sets. About 4 minutes per stage.
- This is a suggested practice dose. It is not proven by research.

## Coaching cues

- "Turn first, then run."
- "Run beside the ball, not under it."
- "Call it early."

## Common mistakes

- Backpedaling more than a step or two - turn the hips and run forward. Backpedaling is slow and raises the risk of falling.
- Running straight under the ball - run beside its path so the ball stays in front or to the side at contact.
- Looking down while running - track the ball over your shoulder.
- Trying to hit a hard overhead off a deep lob while off balance - play a high, safe ball and recover.
- Silent partners - make one clear call on every lob.

## Versions

- Easier: Stay with the no-ball rehearsal and predictable feeds. The feeder lobs lower and shorter.
- Harder: Random lob feeds mixed with occasional short dinks so the player cannot leave early. With a doubles partner, add random "switch" situations.

## Success check

- Count lobs out of 10 where the player turns and runs without backpedaling more than one step, then returns the ball in play.
- Practice goal: 7 out of 10.
- With a doubles partner, also count clear calls out of 10. Practice goal: 10 out of 10.
