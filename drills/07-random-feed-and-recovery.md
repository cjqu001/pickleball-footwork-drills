# FW-07: Random Feed and Recovery

## Overview

- ID: FW-07
- Movement skill: Reading a random feed, choosing a movement, and recovering for the next ball.
- Purpose: Combine split step, shuffle, forward steps, and recovery in an unpredictable pattern that is closer to real points.
- Experience level: Players who can complete [FW-02](02-kitchen-lateral-shuffle.md) and [FW-04](04-transition-stop-and-reset.md) with good balance.
- Format: Partner.
- Equipment: Paddle and balls.
- Court area: Front half of the player's side, from about 2 steps behind the kitchen line up to the kitchen line. The feeder is at their kitchen line.

## Setup

- The player starts in ready position about 2 steps behind their kitchen line, near the middle of one half of the court.
- The feeder stands at their own kitchen line with a supply of balls.
- The feeder mixes four soft feed types:
  - Short to the left. Lands in the kitchen, 1 to 2 steps to the left.
  - Short to the right. Lands in the kitchen, 1 to 2 steps to the right.
  - Short and in front. Lands in the kitchen, straight ahead. The player must move forward.
  - At the feet. Lands near the player's feet. The player must hold position and reset.
- The feeder moves along their kitchen line every few feeds.

## Steps

1. Start in ready position.
2. Split step as the feeder contacts the ball.
3. Read the feed and choose a movement:
   - Short to the side - shuffle.
   - Short in front - small steps forward, stop behind the kitchen line.
   - At the feet - stay, bend low, and play a soft reset.
4. Hit a soft dink or reset back to the feeder.
5. Recover to a spot that faces the feeder's current position. If you moved forward, stay at the kitchen line rather than backing up.
6. Stop balanced before the feeder's next contact.
7. A set ends after the planned number of feeds. Walk back to the start spot for the next set.

## Suggested practice dose

- 3 sets of 10 random feeds.
- 45 to 60 seconds rest between sets.
- About 6 minutes total.
- This is a suggested practice dose. It is not proven by research.

## Coaching cues

- "Read, then move."
- "Recover to where the next ball comes from."
- "Set before they hit."

## Common mistakes

- Guessing early and moving before the feed - wait for the split step, then move.
- Always recovering to the same spot - look at where the feeder is and recover to face them.
- Backing up after moving forward - hold the kitchen line once you get there.
- Rushing the shot because the next feed is coming - the feeder waits for a balanced stop. Speed comes later.
- Stepping into the kitchen on a forward feed - stop behind the line and let the ball bounce.

## Versions

- Easier: Use only two feed types (short left and short right). The feeder stays in one spot.
- Harder: Add a doubles partner beside the player. The feeder alternates feeds between both players. Both players shift together toward the side the ball is on, so the middle stays covered.

## Success check

- Count reps out of 10 where the player chose a sensible movement, made a controlled return, and stopped balanced before the next feed.
- Practice goal: 6 out of 10 to start. Raise it to 8 out of 10 before speeding up the feeds.
