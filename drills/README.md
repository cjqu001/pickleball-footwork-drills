# Drill Index

Every drill uses the same card layout from the [drill template](../templates/drill-template.md). Terms are explained in [footwork basics](../docs/footwork-basics.md).

| ID | Drill | Skill | Solo or partner | Equipment | Provenance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FW-01 | [Split-step timing](01-split-step-timing.md) | Timing a ready hop to opponent contact | Solo rehearsal, partner test | Paddle, balls | Original drill |
| FW-02 | [Kitchen lateral shuffle](02-kitchen-lateral-shuffle.md) | Short side-to-side moves at the kitchen line | Partner (solo rehearsal) | Paddle, balls, 2 optional markers | Original adaptation of S2 |
| FW-03 | [Adjustment steps and spacing](03-adjustment-steps-and-spacing.md) | Small steps to get a good contact distance | Partner (solo rehearsal) | Paddle, balls | Original drill |
| FW-04 | [Transition stop and reset](04-transition-stop-and-reset.md) | Moving forward and stopping balanced | Partner (solo rehearsal) | Paddle, balls, 3 optional markers | Original adaptation of S2 |
| FW-05 | [Crossover and recovery](05-crossover-and-recovery.md) | Choosing shuffle or crossover by distance | Partner (solo rehearsal) | Paddle, balls, 4 optional markers | Original drill |
| FW-06 | [Turn and retrieve lobs](06-turn-and-retrieve-lobs.md) | Turning and running back for a lob | Solo rehearsal, partner feeds | Paddle, balls, 1 optional marker | Original drill |
| FW-07 | [Random feed and recovery](07-random-feed-and-recovery.md) | Reading a feed, moving, and recovering | Partner | Paddle, balls | Original adaptation of S2 |

## Suggested order

- New to footwork drills: FW-01, FW-02, FW-03, then FW-04.
- Comfortable with those: add FW-05 and FW-06.
- Ready to test reactions: FW-07.

## Rehearsal and reaction

- Each card starts with predictable work.
- Most cards end with a random or partner-read version.
- Do not skip straight to random feeds if the predictable version is still unbalanced.
