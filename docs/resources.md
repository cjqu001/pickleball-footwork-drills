## S2: Pickleball Coaches Manual

- Title: Pickleball Coaches Manual (file name: pickleball_coaches_fast_track_manual.pdf)
- Author or organization: Pickleball International / International Coaches Association
- URL: https://www.pickleball-international.org/uploads/1/9/0/2/19023363/pickleball_coaches_fast_track_manual.pdf
- Footwork section: https://www.pickleball-international.org/uploads/1/9/0/2/19023363/pickleball_coaches_fast_track_manual.pdf#page=31
- Format: Public PDF, 161 PDF pages when checked
- Access status: Excerpt reviewed
- Date checked: November 18, 2026
- Sections reviewed: The footwork section only, PDF viewer pages 30 to 37. Page 30 is the section title. Pages 31 to 37 cover footwork principles, lateral open-stance and closed-stance drills, forward and backward movement drills, and combined movement drills. PDF page 33 repeats the content of page 32. Page numbers are PDF viewer numbers, not printed numbers.
- Relevant skill: Lateral, forward, backward, and combined movement progressions.
- Main idea used here: Learn a movement with a caught ball first, then progress to paddle contact.
- Note on court geometry: Some manual drills start at the baseline and progress to dinks. This repository does not copy that setup. Our dink drills use feeds from the kitchen area.
- License note: Free to read online does not mean openly licensed. This repository links to the PDF. It does not include the PDF, its images, or long passages.
